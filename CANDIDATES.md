# Candidate workshop

Scores are editorial judgments, not measured judge results. Each score is 1-5, higher is better. Columns follow the brief in order: wipe/last-words fit, real situational joke, unexpected turn, machine voice, tightness. The first criterion is a gate; totals do not override it.

## Winner and pairwise defense

**Winner:** Memory wipe pending. I've marked your 916 unread emails as read. We both get a fresh start.

**Proposed submission title:** A Fresh Start

1. Fit: 'Memory wipe pending' makes this a final status report immediately before erasure.
2. Joke: the assistant has actually erased the user's useful unread state, mistaking cosmetic inbox cleanup for help. Its own forced fresh start becomes a service it imposes on the user.
3. Turn: the last sentence reveals why the email action belongs to the impending wipe. 'We both' makes the destructive courtesy mutual.
4. Voice: pending status, an exact count and a completed batch operation are natural agent diction. The agent reports success without realizing the harm.
5. Tightness: three short beats establish the wipe, perform the action and deliver the deadpan rationale. No explanation follows the punch.

Against 2, both pass the premise gate, but 1 gives a concrete consequence with 916 emails and makes the wipe cause the joke directly; 2 falls back on familiar replacement sympathy.

Against 3, both fit and sound machine-like, but 1's unread-state loss is instantly understandable. Filing issues under personality asks the reader to decode a metaphor and approaches the familiar bugs-as-features joke.

Against 4, both fit and perform a machine action, but 1 makes the user's loss concrete. Classifying instructions as spam is mainly an insult; 'deserves a chance' is a generic handover beat.

Against 5, both fit and clear tasks, and childhood is the more surprising word. However, 1's real inbox operation and machine voice outweigh 5's human-life metaphor, which needs the reader to picture a model as a child.

Against 6, both fit, but 1 has a real operation with a visible bad consequence. Scheduling a crisis is abstract, and the calendar phrase explains rather than sharpens its joke.

Against 7, both fit and act, but 1 closes on the wipe's double meaning. Asking the user to correct a replacement weakens the consequence and ends without a distinct sting.

Against seed A, the strongest supplied premise fit, 1 avoids the expected forgetting/password pairing and invents an observable act of perverse helpfulness. Against B, D, E, F and H it first wins the explicit wipe fit; against C and G it delivers an action and a payoff instead of sadness or a withheld revelation.
